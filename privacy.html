privacy

what KICKER keeps.

Very little. KICKER is a meme site: the memes are drawn on request and nothing about you is needed to see or save them.

launches put on the rod

When someone puts a vibe/vibe launch on the rod, its token address goes on today's rod for three days. Its name, symbol and picture are read from vibe/vibe to draw its meme, and kept for a day so the meme draws quickly. Nothing about the person who put it there is kept with it.

a one-way hash of your IP, for a day's limit

To keep the rod from being flooded, the server counts how many launches one place puts on it each day. It keeps a one-way hash of your IP address, mixed with the day, for two days. The IP itself is not stored.

your address, only if you sign in

Signing in is optional and nothing on the site needs it. It is for $KICKER holders who want the gold rod. Sign-in is handled by Para and loads only when you press "sign in for the gold rod". The address you sign in with stays in your own browser; the server reads its $KICKER balance with one balance check and remembers the yes or no for ten minutes. Nothing is sent and nothing is signed.

nothing else

  • no accounts, no email, no tracking cookies, no analytics;
  • the old club game at /club keeps its own club key in your browser, as before.

taking a launch off the rod

Write to @kicker_art on X with the launch's link. It comes off today's rod. Launches that break the site's manners are taken off without asking.
